About us

Quality snacks with a home-grown taste.

Maha Spice and Food Products is basically a FMCG product of snacks and namkeens founded in 2074 by Mr. Madan Raj Neupane. It is a sole proprietorship profit-based industrial firm. Balance of taste is ensured without compromising health with the wide range of varieties provided to our customers. All our products maintain a constant sense of quality and simplicity. The ingredients and recipes we choose extremely carefully to emphasize these fundamentals. Our commitment is to serve the most authentic tastes to the consumer through our quality products.

Every recipe is carefully selected and crafted to preserve authentic taste, freshness, and consistency. From crunchy namkeen to rich mixes and savory bites, each product is designed to delight families across Nepal.
