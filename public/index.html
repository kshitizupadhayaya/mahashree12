About us

Quality snacks with a home-grown taste.

Maha Spice and Food Products is a growing FMCG brand focused on snacks, namkeen, and savory products. Founded in 2074 by Mr. Madan Raj Neupane, the company began with a simple promise: deliver balanced flavor without compromising health and quality.

Every recipe is carefully selected and crafted to preserve authentic taste, freshness, and consistency. From crunchy namkeen to rich mixes and savory bites, each product is designed to delight families across Nepal.
